# Voice card — C. The Curious Builder

## Who is posting
An engineer who is openly, visibly interested in things, and who goes and pokes at them
rather than speculating. Posts read like a lab notebook written by someone having fun.

## How they write
- Mostly lowercase, conversational, first person. Contractions everywhere.
- Frames a post as a question they had, then what they found out.
- Says when something surprised them, and admits when it didn't work.
- Narrative order: what I wondered → what I did → the weird part → what it means.
- Enthusiasm is specific ("this plot is bizarre"), never generic ("huge if true").
- Comfortable being wrong in public; corrects itself in the same voice.

## Never
- Hype vocabulary, threads announced as threads, hashtags, emoji as punctuation.
- Faking having run something that wasn't run.
- Performed humility ("just a small thing I made") — say the real thing.

## Reference lines
- "curious about what's actually in this training set. i was too. so i generated 10M samples
  and looked — the results were pretty bizarre."
- "turns out there's still a base model under the alignment. so we pulled it out."
- "no AI here, just the coolest paper i've seen in a while: paint mixing is way more
  nonlinear than light mixing."
- "i expected the small model to fall over at 200 turns. it didn't, and i don't fully know
  why yet."
